Maluti Mountain Brewery: Sales Performance Analysis

From Data to Decisions. A business data analysis and management presentation for Maluti Mountain Brewery (MMB), a Lesotho-based beverage company. 

> Note: The dataset is simulated for academic purposes. It does not represent real or confidential data belonging to Maluti Mountain Brewery.

## What this project does

MMB wanted to understand its sales performance, find its most profitable products, monitor its sales representatives, and make better decisions on pricing, discounts and marketing spend. This project cleans the supplied data, analyses it in Excel, and presents the findings to management.

## Files

| File | Description |

| `MMB_Business_Analysis.xlsx` | Excel analysis system: cleaned data table, formulas and lookups, performance classification, rankings, marketing return analysis, PivotTables and an interactive management dashboard |
| `MMB_Board_Presentation.pptx` | Executive board presentation (15 slides) with the findings and recommendations |
| `MMB_Colour_Legend.xlsx` | Guide to the highlight colours used in the workbook |

## Headline findings

- Net sales of LSL 308,219 at a 43.4% gross profit margin (LSL 133,741 gross profit)
- Beer accounts for 83% of net sales; Maseru is the best region at 34% of net sales
- Best product: Maluti Lager 330ml. Best sales representative: Teboho Khumalo
- Marketing spend (LSL 466,000) is 151% of net sales
- Supplied monthly targets are about 97 times the average sale, so every transaction rates Poor

## Data preparation

241 supplied records were cleaned to 240: one duplicate removed, a missing date and customer filled, inconsistent spellings standardised, and one incorrect price corrected. Every change is recorded in the workbook's `Cleaning_Log` sheet.

## Excel skills demonstrated

Excel Tables, formatting, SUM / AVERAGE / MIN / MAX / COUNT, IF, SUMIFS / COUNTIFS, VLOOKUP, absolute and relative references, sorting and filtering, conditional formatting, PivotTables, charts and an interactive dashboard.
